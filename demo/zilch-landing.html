FAQs

Fair questions

How is it actually free?

Text ads fund the compute. No metered tokens, no seat pricing, no paid tier where the good model hides.

What's the catch with ads?

Ads appear as text in the chat sidebar — never injected into your code, your prompts, or your agent's context.

Do I need an API key or a card?

No, and no. Install, sign in, build.

Can I leave? Will I lose my work?

Your code lives in your repos, not in Zilch. Every product syncs to GitHub — leaving is a checkout command away. No lock-in is the whole point.
