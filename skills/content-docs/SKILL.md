# Documentation and content work

Use the supplied source of truth and preserve uncertainty. A polished document must not invent prices, dates, policy, product claims, contacts, or operational steps.

## Working rules

- Identify the audience, purpose, owner, and authoritative source before drafting.
- Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and unanswered questions.
- Use actionable steps with prerequisites, expected results, and recovery guidance for runbooks.
- Keep terminology, links, and cross-references consistent.
- Use placeholders for missing facts and flag them for review rather than filling them from memory.
- Review the final document for completeness, readability, and unintended claims.
